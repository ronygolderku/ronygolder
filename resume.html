Summary

Md Rony Golder, PhD (Under Examination)

Research Fellow at The University of Western Australia specializing in ocean color remote sensing, biological oceanography, and marine ecosystem modeling. PhD candidate in Spatial Science with 27 research outputs including 18 peer-reviewed articles (h-index: 7, 95 citations - Scopus). Core expertise: water quality assessment, TUFLOW/AED model validation, satellite data analysis, GIS, and marine biogeochemical processes. Previously: Consultant at BMT, Research Officer at UWA, Data Analyst at WAMSI/SEAF, Casual Academic at Curtin University, Visiting Fellow at Université de Brest, and Research Intern at CSIRO.
